About Us

ShopHub is an online marketplace where sellers list their products and buyers in Rwanda can browse, add items to a cart and order with delivery to their door.

What we offer

  • A growing catalog from independent sellers.
  • Clear prices in Rwandan francs, with taxes included.
  • Flexible payment: card, MTN MoMo or cash on delivery.
  • A secure account for every buyer and seller.
