Privacy Policy

Sample policy for this demo store. Replace it with your own before real use.

What we collect

Your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order history, so we can create your account and deliver your orders. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes - we cannot read them.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, send order confirmations and password-reset emails, and answer your messages. We do not sell your personal data.

Payments

We never store card numbers. Card and mobile-money payments are handled by the payment provider.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in and to protect forms. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can update your name and password in My Account, or ask us to delete your account through the Contact page.
